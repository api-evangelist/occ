The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, and the National Credit Union Association (collectively, the agencies) today issued guidance to remind supervised financial institutions of existing supervisory expectations with respect to credit risk management, particularly as it relates to borrowers who are not legally authorized to work in the United States.
