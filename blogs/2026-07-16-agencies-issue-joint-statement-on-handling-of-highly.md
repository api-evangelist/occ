Agencies Issue Joint Statement on Handling of Highly Sensitive Information During Bank Examinations The federal bank regulatory agencies today issued a joint statement describing enhanced security procedures for review of highly sensitive information in connection with examinations of supervised banks, such as reviewing materials on-site rather than transferring them onto agency systems.
