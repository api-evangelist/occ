The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) reported cumulative trading revenue of U.S. commercial banks and savings associations of $21.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The second quarter trading revenue was $5.3 billion, or 32.5 percent, more than in the previous quarter and $5.1 billion, or 30.6 percent, more than a year earlier.
